Your privacy is important. This page explains what this website collects, how it's used, and your rights.

1. Information Collected

When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, selected service, and message. We also track anonymous visit counts stored in Firebase.

2. How We Use It

  • To respond to your project enquiry within 24 hours
  • To send you an automatic confirmation email
  • We do not sell or share your information with third parties

3. Firebase (Data Storage)

Messages and project data are stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore. Admin access requires Firebase Authentication — only the site owner can view messages. See Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

  • Consent cookie: Saves your cookie preference in your browser
  • Google Analytics: Anonymous traffic data — opt out here

5. Email

If you contact us, you'll receive an automatic confirmation email. Emails are sent via Brevo. You can opt out by replying to any email. See Brevo Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You can request your data, ask for deletion, or opt out at any time. Contact: ademujimidaniel4@gmail.com
